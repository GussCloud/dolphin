# Dolphin v0.1.17

## Fixes

- **Terminals no longer close by themselves when a worktree's path is written two ways.** A workspace listed as `C:\work\repo` in one place and `c:/work/repo/` in another was treated as removed, and its open terminals were stopped. The two spellings now count as the same workspace.

Update from inside Dolphin, or reinstall with `dolphin-windows-setup.exe`.
