# Dolphin v0.1.43

## New

- **Sign in to Dolphin with Azure DevOps.** When you are not signed in to Dolphin (and did not sign out on purpose), the organization check signs you in with your Azure DevOps account. The Azure DevOps card also has a "Sign in with Azure DevOps" button.
  - The first sign-in creates a Dolphin account if your e-mail is not in use. If it is, you are asked to sign in to Dolphin once instead, and the two are never merged automatically.
  - Members who linked before this release are recognized automatically.

Update from inside Dolphin, or reinstall with `dolphin-windows-setup.exe`.
