# Dolphin v0.1.44

## Improvements

- **The organization console has a new look.** Sign-in and sign-up pages are redesigned and work on phones, and signed-in pages have a collapsible sidebar.
- **Members page in the console:** search by name, e-mail or join date, give members a nickname for the organization, and remove members (the owner is protected; someone removed rejoins by signing in with Azure DevOps).

## Fixes

- **The console's member date filter ignores impossible dates** instead of failing.

Update from inside Dolphin, or reinstall with `dolphin-windows-setup.exe`.
