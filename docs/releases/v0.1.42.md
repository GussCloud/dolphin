# Dolphin v0.1.42

## New

- **Dolphin organizations for Azure DevOps teams.** The Azure DevOps card in settings shows whether your Azure DevOps organization belongs to a Dolphin organization, with a "Check now" action. Your Azure DevOps token is checked for each request and never stored.
  - A "not registered" answer is re-checked after 10 minutes, and the check runs again after you sign in to Dolphin.
  - If the organization has public projects and your token lacks the "Project and team (Read)" scope, the card says so.
  - Remote hosts show the link as unavailable, since it is proven with this computer's sign-in.
- **Organization web console** where admins sign up by invite, connect their Azure DevOps organization and see members.

## Fixes

- **Only real people who belong to the Azure DevOps organization can join its Dolphin organization.** Anonymous access to public projects and service accounts are no longer accepted as proof of membership.

Update from inside Dolphin, or reinstall with `dolphin-windows-setup.exe`.
