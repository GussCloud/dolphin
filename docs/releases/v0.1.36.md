# Dolphin v0.1.36

## Fixes

- **A closed Agent Teams teammate no longer comes back as a lone Claude session.** When the lead closed a teammate while its workspace was not on screen, the pane stayed saved and, on the next visit, reopened with `claude --resume` outside the team. It is now removed.

Update from inside Dolphin, or reinstall with `dolphin-windows-setup.exe`.
